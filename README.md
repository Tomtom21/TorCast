What is TorCast?
---
TorCast is a severe weather prediction tool that aims to forecast the location and number of severe storm reports across the continental US based on atmospheric data. While current forecasting methods are highly capable in accurately forecasting servere weather events, 

Modern severe weather forecasting is highly capable in a large majority of situations. However, from time to time, "busts" occur where high intensity setups, predicted by forecasters and other models, fail to validate and lack development or intensity. While the lack of severe weather is obviously good news, it can often lead to fatigue in the population where people stop listening to weather alerts, believing them to be false. The idea behind this project is to hopefully provide another set of eyes, trained on years of atmospheric data, to supplement forecasting efforts.

Input Data
---
RAP and Historical RAP are the datasets of choice, since they provide a decent amount of history data while offering near real-time information for forecasting on current conditions.
